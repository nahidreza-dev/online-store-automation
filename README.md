## The problem

Most online store owners in Bangladesh, and many abroad, spend the day doing the same jobs by hand: answering the same questions on Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp, calling customers to confirm cash-on-delivery orders, booking couriers, catching fake orders, and adding up profit in a spreadsheet.

## The solution

One system where the store, the automation and the owner's dashboard work together. The owner adds products. The system handles the rest and only asks for help when a human is really needed.

## Features

**Storefront:** mobile-first design · USD and BDT markets · product pages with size guide · colour and size selection, with a clear warning if either is missing · wishlist · search · discount codes · order tracking · newsletter sign-up · sold-out "notify me"

**Payments and delivery:** cash on delivery, bKash, Nagad, cards (Bangladesh) · cards, PayPal, Apple Pay (worldwide) · Steadfast, Pathao, RedX, DHL, UPS, Royal Mail

**Automation:** AI replies on Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and website · automatic confirmation calls · fake-order protection · courier booking and tracking SMS · abandoned-bag, review, reorder and restock campaigns · owner alerts on WhatsApp and Telegram

**Owner dashboard (11 sections):** overview · orders · products · customers · AI inbox · confirmation calls · delivery · marketing · profit reports · automations · settings

**Documents:** invoices and courier labels (print or PDF) · receipts · CSV export of orders and customers · monthly PDF report
